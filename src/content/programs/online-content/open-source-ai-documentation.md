Open documentation turns individual technical experience into shared infrastructure. This resource collection focuses on the difficult space between a successful demonstration and a dependable production system.

Every guide prioritizes explicit assumptions, observable behavior, and recoverable failure modes. Examples are intentionally compact so readers can understand the whole system before adapting it.
