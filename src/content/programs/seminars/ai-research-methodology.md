AI can reduce the administrative cost of research, but it can also produce convincing errors at scale. This seminar teaches researchers to use generative systems as structured collaborators while preserving source integrity and scholarly accountability.

Participants work through realistic research scenarios, compare verification approaches, and leave with a documented workflow they can adapt to their own discipline.
