## Prerequisites

Students should have prior knowledge of probability and statistics, calculus, and linear algebra. Familiarity with a high-level programming language such as C, C++, or Python is also expected for understanding data science concepts and completing projects.

## References

- Steven S. Skiena, _The Data Science Design Manual_. Springer Cham, 2017.
- Cathy O’Neil and Rachel Schutt, _Doing Data Science_. O’Reilly Media, Inc., 2013.
- Joel Grus, _Data Science from Scratch, 2nd Edition: First Principles with Python_. O’Reilly Media, 2019.
- Ivo D. Dinov, _Data Science and Predictive Analytics_. Springer, 2018.
