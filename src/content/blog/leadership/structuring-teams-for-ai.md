AI products expose the limits of traditional team boundaries. A model can be technically strong and operationally weak; a polished interface can conceal uncertain behavior. Successful teams bring the necessary perspectives into one continuous delivery loop.

## Organize around decisions

Instead of grouping work by technical specialty, organize around the decision or workflow the system supports. Give each team access to domain experts, product leadership, data and software engineering, evaluation, and risk stewardship.

## Create a common language

Teams move faster when they share definitions for quality. Establish a compact scorecard that includes task success, user trust, latency, cost, safety, and recovery behavior. This makes trade-offs visible before they become production incidents.

The objective is not a larger AI department. It is an organization in which AI expertise can travel to the work that needs it.
