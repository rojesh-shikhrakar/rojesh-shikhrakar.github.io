AI gives small teams access to capabilities that once required a much larger organization. The advantage does not come from subscribing to more tools. It comes from understanding the work well enough to direct those tools coherently.

## Find the repeated decision

Look for work that recurs, consumes attention, and follows a recognizable pattern. Document its inputs, judgment points, outputs, and failure modes. This creates a practical map for deciding what an AI system should prepare, recommend, or execute.

## Build the smallest useful loop

Begin with one workflow and a clear success measure. Keep the human decision visible, capture corrections, and improve the system using real operating evidence.

For founders, AI advantage is ultimately an organizational design advantage: a small team with clear workflows can learn and compound faster than a larger team with fragmented tools.
