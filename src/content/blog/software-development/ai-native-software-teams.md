AI can compress the time required to produce a first implementation. It does not compress the responsibility required to understand, test, and maintain that implementation.

## Shift attention to the specification

When implementation becomes cheaper, ambiguity becomes more expensive. Teams benefit from smaller interfaces, explicit acceptance criteria, and examples of expected behavior before generating code.

## Invest in verification

Tests, observability, type systems, and review practices are not overhead in an AI-native workflow. They are the mechanisms that let increased implementation speed become sustainable delivery speed.

The strongest teams will not be those that generate the most code. They will be those that can determine quickly which code belongs in the system.
