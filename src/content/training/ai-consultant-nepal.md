## Independent AI advice for organizations in Nepal

Many organizations in Nepal are under pressure to "do something with AI" but lack a clear view of where it helps, what it risks and who will own it. Consulting engagements answer those questions first, then build the internal skills to act on them.

Rojesh Man Shikhrakar brings an unusual combination to this work: hands-on machine learning engineering, a decade of designing AI education programs at Fusemachines, university teaching at Kathmandu University, and AI programs for organizations such as Nepal Rastra Bank, UNDP, Plan International, the National Inclusion Commission, the Government of Nepal (MoWCSC), and the Armed Police Force Academy. That mix keeps recommendations technically sound and realistic about how people actually adopt new tools.
