## Improve the work, not just the prompt

Participants practice with common knowledge-work tasks and learn how to decide when AI helps, how to evaluate quality and how to turn a successful experiment into a reusable workflow.

> The goal is a dependable working method, with professional judgment and verification kept in the loop.
